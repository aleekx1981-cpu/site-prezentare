FAQ

How long does setup take?

Your free pilot starts within 1–2 days.

Do I need to change anything in my email or my routine?

No. You keep using everything you use today. You just give me access to your email address, and I set up the rest.

Do the replies go out on their own? Who handles special requests?

Yes, replies go out automatically, in under a minute. Special cases, such as availability or unusual requests, are flagged in the sheet with "needs owner: yes", so you decide. The automatic reply never confirms a booking.

What happens after the free pilot?

If you're happy with the results, we continue with a subscription from €80/month (excl. VAT). If not, we simply stop — no obligation, no cost.
